## Place your first bet

### Step 1 — Log in

Open the app and click **Sign in** in the top-right navbar. Auth0 handles authentication. If this is your first visit, you will land on the **UsernameModal** after login — pick a username (3–20 chars, letters/numbers/underscores) before continuing. You start with 1000 bits.

> You can browse and simulate bets while signed out. Clicking **Buy** while signed out opens a signup prompt.

### Step 2 — Find a package

The app opens on **Predict**. Search for a package by name and add it to your slip. To browse first, use the **PyPI** and **npm** tabs and expand a row for details.

![Predict page with an empty slip: search bar, Vulnerability happens / No vulnerability toggle, stake chips, and 7d/14d/30d duration](https://assets.polydelve.com/screenshots/predict-empty.png)

![PyPI package table with cryptography expanded, showing its CVE list and EPSS trend chart](https://assets.polydelve.com/screenshots/packages-pypi.png)

### Step 3 — Gauge risk with the EPSS chart

The EPSS Trend chart plots daily EPSS scores as a green line; dots mark past CVE publication dates, colored by CVSS severity. It appears when you add a package to your slip, and when you expand a row in the PyPI or npm tables.

![Expanded package row: CVE list with severity and score beside the EPSS trend chart](https://assets.polydelve.com/screenshots/package-epss.png)

- **Higher EPSS** means the package is considered more likely to be exploited, so a win is more likely and the payout multiplier is lower.
- A rising trend line signals increasing exploitation risk.

Use the chart to decide whether the payout is worth the risk before committing bits.

### Step 4 — Configure and buy

On the Predict page:

1. Search for a package and add it to **Your slip**.
2. Choose **Vulnerability happens** (YES) or **No vulnerability** (NO; only for packages with a CVE in the last 100 days).
3. Set the CVSS threshold, and for a single-package YES bet an EPSS scenario target. Pick a stake (chips of 25, 100, 250, 500, or type an amount; minimum 10 bits) and a duration (7d, 14d, or 30d).
4. Watch **If it hits**, **Win chance**, and **Max loss** update. Adding more packages turns the slip into a basket with a **Legs that must hit** slider.

![Predict slip with axios: 1 search, 2 pick a stake, 3 choose duration; If it hits, Win chance, and Max loss shown](https://assets.polydelve.com/screenshots/predict-slip.png)

When satisfied, click **Buy slip for N bits**. The stake is deducted immediately.

### Step 5 — Track your position

Go to **Dashboard** (avatar menu) to see your open contracts, balance over time, and per-contract P&L. Check the **Leaderboard** to see where you rank against other players.

![Dashboard: open contracts, total invested, current value, unrealized P&L, balance over time, active contracts and history](https://assets.polydelve.com/screenshots/dashboard.png)

![Leaderboard table with bits, contracts, open, won, and win rate](https://assets.polydelve.com/screenshots/leaderboard.png)

## What happens next

Contracts resolve automatically — no manual action required. Resolution runs three times a day (9am, 12pm, 5pm ET).

| Bet | Outcome | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| YES | **Won** | A new CVE at or above your CVSS threshold, an EPSS crossing of your target, or a MAL advisory, before the deadline | Payout credited to your bits balance |
| YES | **Expired** | Deadline passes with none of those | No payout; stake is not refunded |
| NO | **Won** | Deadline passes with no CVE at or above your threshold | Payout credited |
| NO | **Lost** | A qualifying CVE is published before the deadline | No payout |

Check the **Dashboard** and **Leaderboard** after the next run to see updated balances.
