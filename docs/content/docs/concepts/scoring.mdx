## How the leaderboard works

There is no per-user prediction-accuracy or calibration score. The leaderboard ranks users strictly by **bits balance** — `ORDER BY bits DESC` (`features/users_repo.py`).

Balance moves in two ways:

- **Buying a contract** — `purchase_price` is deducted immediately.
- **Won contract** — the holder's balance increases by `max_payout`. This covers YES contracts that hit a win condition and NO contracts that survive to expiry.
- **Expired or lost contract** — no payout. A YES contract that reaches its deadline is `expired`; a NO contract hit by a qualifying CVE is `lost`.

There is no separate "accuracy" or "calibration" metric anywhere in the schema or API. Payouts are fair odds, so there is no bonus for having bought in at a worse implied probability.

## Leaderboard

The leaderboard is served from `GET /users/leaderboard` (public, paginated, with a `search` parameter) and rendered by the `LeaderboardTable` component. Each row shows rank, username, bits balance, total contracts, open contracts, won contracts, and a win rate (won / total). Clicking a row expands that user's recent contracts.

Users with zero resolved contracts still appear on the leaderboard — ranking is purely by balance, so a new user (starting at 1000 bits) is ranked alongside everyone else.
